## The interrupted OpenAI Rust extension

The original stock 68d5 Nanoda binary aborted with worker stack overflow; its compiled requested worker stack was 16 MiB. That [failed recording](openai/native-phase-records/r2-06-extra-nanoda/phase-result.json) remains preserved. A separately hashed resource build changes only the requested worker-stack constant to 256 MiB, retaining the strict two-thread configuration, three permitted axioms, Nat/String extensions and selected export. The [29-file provenance](verification-tools-worker-stack256/source-provenance-r1.json) records the one-constant source difference; no checker-logic patch or another fork's acceptance is borrowed. Actual worker stack extent was not measured. An intermediate exact-environment preflight failure and unsupported main-stack controls are also retained.

The resource attempt's [result](openai/native-retry-records/worker-stack256-r3-02/retry-result.json) records signal15 and a PermissionError at 17:45:40 UTC. It has an empty stage-receipt list, no finished stage or terminal checker record, and an empty outer JSON capture. The [later recovery observation](openai/logs/interruption-recovery-observation-r1.json) found the known PID/group absent and bound the surviving small files and missing records. **This was an interruption, not a deadline timeout.** Its trigger, checker exit, acceptance, final attempt CPU/wall and full campaign totals remain unknown. No logical rejection is inferred.

Recovered raw fragments are labelled `recovered_log`, outside the completed-log collection. The zero-byte outer capture remains local with its exact empty SHA/size in observation metadata; it is neither parsed nor published as a completed JSON receipt. Final postidentity passed at 18:01:58 UTC, after recovery, and the separate interrupted-evidence closure validated all seven original successful phases.
